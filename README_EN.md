## About Me

I have an academic background in health sciences and sports science, including a PhD in Exercise Physiology, and extensive experience from higher education, healthcare, and the public sector.

Throughout my career, I have worked with research, teaching, supervision, project development, and health promotion initiatives. I am particularly interested in how technology, data, and automation can be used to create better services, more efficient workflows, and improved decision-making.

This GitHub profile is used to document projects related to data analysis, automation, digitalization, and Linux.
